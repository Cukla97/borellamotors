# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: private individuals selling a used car for the first time — often uncertain about value, process, and paperwork. Secondary: people already comparing offers from other dealers or portals, looking for a clearer local alternative.

Context is usually mobile, between errands or after deciding “it’s time to sell.” The job to be done is to get a serious purchase proposal without listing publicly or navigating a complex marketplace.

## Product Purpose

A lead-generation landing for Borella Motors that lets visitors request a free vehicle valuation: enter car details, optionally add photos, submit, and wait for the dealer to call or email with an offer off-platform. No payments, no user accounts.

Success is high form completion with enough signal (complete data, preferably photos) that low-quality or unserious leads are filtered out by the form itself.

## Positioning

Sell without the hassle — if you accept, we handle everything.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Richiedi una valutazione gratuita
- Secondary CTA: call or contact the dealership directly
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Valuti gratis, se accetti pensiamo a tutto noi
- Belief ladder: the valuation is free and no-obligation → a local dealer will respond with a real proposal → if they accept, Borella handles the rest → submitting the form is the safe next step
- Proof on hand: soft claims only for now (free valuation, local seat). No hard numbers or reviews yet. Company contacts and address to be added when available.

## Brand Personality

Locale, affidabile, concreto. Voice is clear and practical, never pushy. The interface should feel like a trusted neighborhood dealer: confidence and relief without sales pressure.

## Anti-references

Must not look like a cold corporate site from a large dealer chain — impersonal, glossy, distant from the local owner-operated reality.

## Design Principles

- Local and concrete over glossy corporate polish
- Trust and relief without urgency tactics or hard sell
- One clear path to the valuation form; every section earns its place by reducing doubt
- Form is the product: clarity and completion beat clever marketing
- Show the human dealer process, not a marketplace or algorithm

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Solid practical accessibility for form and mobile use: associated labels, visible focus, readable contrast, touch-friendly targets, and respect for reduced motion. No formal WCAG certification target for v1.
